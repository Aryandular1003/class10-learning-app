Question 12 (Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations)
Question: Find the discriminant of the quadratic equation 2x2 −4x+3 = 0 and hence find the
nature of its roots.
Prediction Reason: Core discriminant problem from Section B.
Detailed Solution: Comparing with ax2+bx+c = 0: a = 2,b = −4,c = 3.

Since D < 0, the quadratic equation has no real roots (complex/imaginary roots).

Question 16 (Chapter 10: Circles)
Question: Prove that the parallelogram circumscribing a circle is a rhombus. (Verified 2024
Board PYQ)
Prediction Reason: High-frequency PYQ theorem application.
Detailed Solution: Let ABCD be a parallelogram circumscribing a circle touching at P,Q,R,S.
Tangents from external points are equal:
AP = AS, BP = BQ, CR = CQ, DR = DS
Adding all four: (AP +BP)+(CR+DR) = (AS +DS)+(BQ+CQ)
AB+CD = AD+BC
Since ABCD is a parallelogram, AB = CD and AD = BC:
2AB = 2AD =⇒ AB = AD
Parallelogram with equal adjacent sides is a rhombus.
