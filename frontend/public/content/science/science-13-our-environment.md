## SECTION 2: Complete Syllabus-Aligned Revision Notes

Chapter 13: Our Environment [5 Marks Marks | Unit V]
1. Ecosystem Structure: Self-sustaining unit of biotic (producers, consumers,
decomposers) and abiotic components (temp, soil, water). Natural (forest, pond) vs
Artificial (aquarium, crop field). Decomposers (bacteria/fungi) enable nutrient cycling.
2. Food Chain & 10% Energy Law: Producers capture 1% solar energy. Lindeman's 10%
Law: Only 10% energy transfers to next higher trophic level; 90% lost as metabolic
heat. Food chains limited to 3-4 steps.
3. Biological Magnification: Progressive accumulation and increasing concentration of
non-biodegradable toxic chemicals (e.g., DDT) at higher trophic levels (maximum in
human top carnivores).

UV radiation (O₂ + UV → O + O; O + O₂ → O₃). Depleted by Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs,
