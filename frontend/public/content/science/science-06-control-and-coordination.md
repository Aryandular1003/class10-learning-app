## SECTION 2: Complete Syllabus-Aligned Revision Notes

Chapter 6: Control and Coordination [6 Marks Marks | Unit II]
1. Nervous System & Reflex Arc: Neuron structure (Dendrite → Cell Body → Axon →
Synaptic Terminal). Synapse is the chemical gap crossed by neurotransmitters. Reflex
Arc: Receptor → Sensory Neuron → Spinal Cord (Relay Neuron) → Motor Neuron →
Effector Muscle.
2. Human Brain: Forebrain (Cerebrum: main thinking area, memory), Midbrain
(involuntary actions), Hindbrain (Cerebellum: posture/balance; Medulla: blood
pressure, salvation, vomiting; Pons: respiration rate).
3. Plant Hormones (Phytohormones): Auxin (cell elongation, phototropism bending
toward light), Gibberellin (stem growth), Cytokinin (cell division in fruits/seeds),
Abscisic Acid (stress hormone, inhibits growth, wilting of leaves). Plant movements:
Tropic (directional: photo-, geo-, hydro-, chemo-) vs Nastic (non-directional: *Mimosa
pudica* touch response).
4. Human Endocrine Glands & Hormones: Pituitary (Growth Hormone), Thyroid
(Thyroxin - requires iodine to prevent Goitre), Pancreas (Insulin - regulates blood
sugar, prevents Diabetes), Adrenal (Adrenaline - fight or flight hormone), Testes
(Testosterone), Ovaries (Estrogen).
