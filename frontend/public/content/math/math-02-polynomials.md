Chapter 2: Polynomials (Weightage: 4 Marks)
Core Concepts & Zeroes Relationship:
1. Zeroes of a Polynomial: A real number k is a zero of polynomial p(x) if p(k) = 0. Geometrically, zeroes are the
x-coordinates of points where y = p(x) intersects the x-axis.
2. Quadratic Parabola: The graph of ax² + bx + c is a parabola opening upwards (a > 0) or downwards (a < 0).
KEY FORMULA / RESULT: POLYNOMIAL ZEROES & COEFFICIENTS
• For Quadratic Polynomial p(x) = ax² + bx + c:
• • Sum of Zeroes: α + β = -b/a = -(Coefficient of x) / (Coefficient of x²)
• • Product of Zeroes: α · β = c/a = (Constant term) / (Coefficient of x²)
• • Formation of Quadratic Polynomial: p(x) = k [x² - (α + β)x + α·β]
EXAM PRO-TIP / COMMON MISTAKE: Do not confuse factors (x - α) with zeroes α. If zeroes are 2 and -3, the polynomial
is x² - (2 - 3)x + (2)(-3) = x² + x - 6.
