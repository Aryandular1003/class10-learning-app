Chapter 6: Triangles (Weightage: 4 Marks)
Key Geometric Theorems & Similarity Rules:
1. Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT / Thales Theorem): If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the
other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio. (If DE || BC in ΔABC, then AD/DB =
AE/EC).
2. Converse of BPT: If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.
3. Criteria for Similarity: AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle), SSS (Side-Side-Side), SAS (Side-Angle-Side).
