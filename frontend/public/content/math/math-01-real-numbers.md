## SECTION 2: Complete Syllabus-Aligned Revision Notes & Formulas

Chapter 1: Real Numbers (Weightage: 4 Marks)
Core Concepts & Definitions:
1. Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Every composite number can be uniquely expressed as a product of prime
numbers, apart from the order in which the prime factors occur.
2. HCF & LCM Rules: HCF is the product of the smallest powers of each common prime factor. LCM is the product of the
greatest powers of each prime factor involved.
3. Revisiting Irrational Numbers: If a prime number p divides a², then p also divides a (where a is a positive integer).
KEY FORMULA / RESULT: REAL NUMBERS FORMULAS
• HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b (Valid ONLY for two positive integers)
• LCM(a, b) = (a × b) / HCF(a, b)
• Proof of Irrationality: To prove √2, √3, √5 or (a ± b√c) irrational, use Proof by Contradiction assuming √p = a/b
where a, b are co-prime integers (b ≠ 0).
EXAM PRO-TIP / COMMON MISTAKE: Always explicitly state that 'a and b are co-prime integers (b ≠ 0)' during
irrationality proofs to avoid losing 1/2 mark in board exams.
