Chapter 9: Some Applications of Trigonometry (Weightage: 5 Marks)
Heights & Distances Key Definitions:
• Line of Sight: Line drawn from the eye of an observer to the point in the object viewed by the observer.
• Angle of Elevation: Angle formed by the line of sight with the horizontal when the object is above the horizontal level.
• Angle of Depression: Angle formed by the line of sight with the horizontal when the object is below the horizontal level.
EXAM PRO-TIP / COMMON MISTAKE: Angle of Depression = Angle of Elevation (Alternate Interior Angles between
horizontal lines). Always draw a horizontal line at observer's eye level.
