## SECTION 2: Complete Syllabus-Aligned Revision Notes & Formulas

Chapter 11: Areas Related to Circles (Weightage: 5 Marks)
Sector & Segment Formulas:
KEY FORMULA / RESULT: CIRCLE MENSURATION FORMULAS
• Circumference = 2πr, Area of Circle = πr²
• Length of Arc of Sector (angle θ): L = (θ / 360°) × 2πr
• Area of Sector (angle θ): A = (θ / 360°) × πr² OR A = (1/2) × L × r
• Area of Segment: Area of Sector - Area of Corresponding Triangle
• Area of Triangle in Sector: A = (1/2) r² sin θ (For θ = 60°, Δ is equilateral; θ = 90°, Δ is right-angled)
